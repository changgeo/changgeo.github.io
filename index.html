About

I am a Clinical Assistant Professor at Purdue University. My research develops geospatial AI methods that turn satellite imagery, radar and LiDAR point clouds into actionable information about cities and natural hazards, from mapping building damage after earthquakes and floods to revealing the internal structure of informal settlements.

Before joining Purdue, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the MIT Senseable City Lab (2024–2026), where I led the Data Clouds project on LiDAR and satellite-based environmental mapping. I received my Ph.D. from The University of New South Wales Sydney (UNSW) in 2024, supported by a SmartSat CRC scholarship, and hold an M.Phil. from UNSW and a B.E. from The University of Science and Technology Beijing.
